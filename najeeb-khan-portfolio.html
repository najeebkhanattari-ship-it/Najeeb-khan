How I find a fault

  1. Look at the whole panelA fault on one device is often an effect of another. An uncommissioned safety relay can show up as a drive STO fault.
  2. Check what the photos and nameplates sayIdentify the exact device, model and firmware before touching parameters.
  3. Verify power, wiring and safety firstSupply, terminals, STO and safety relay state come before any parameter change.
  4. Change one thing, then testAdjust a parameter or calibration point, run the machine, and confirm the result.
